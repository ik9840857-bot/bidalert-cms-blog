What are Tender Alerts?

Tender alerts are notifications that inform businesses about upcoming government contracts. These alerts provide details about the contract, including the scope of work, eligibility criteria, and bid submission deadlines. Tender alerts can be found on various platforms, including government websites, tendering portals, and third-party alert services. By subscribing to tender alert services, businesses can stay informed about new contract opportunities and plan their bidding strategy accordingly.
